Privacy policy

Werket (ወርቀት) is a private, offline-first writing app. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Documents are stored on your device

Everything you write is saved locally in your browser's storage and never leaves your device. Werket has no accounts, no servers for your documents, and no cloud upload of your text.

Spell check and suggestions

Spell check and word suggestions run on Werket's server so the dictionary stays up to date. Only the words you are currently checking are sent, and they are processed in memory and not stored or logged.

Analytics

Werket does not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project maintainers via the GitHub repository.
